## Problem
The Monster Manual is a **shared mutable cache fought over by ~4 process clones**, which
produces two opposite failure modes seen in the 2026-06-22 playtest sweep:
- **beneath_sunden purge/reseed livelock** — clones purge and reseed each other's pool.
- **0/0 pool vs 1153-NPC runaway pools** — no accumulation cap; per-world seed attribution
  is lost, so pools either never fill or grow without bound.

Root cause (per epic-162 + the inventory spec): no content-sha/session-seed keying, and a
purge-repair strategy that mutates a shared pool instead of deriving a fresh one.

**Authoritative spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-05-npc-generation-inventory.md`
